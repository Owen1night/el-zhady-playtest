# Archive media review — Topic 8 draft

18 original records/files; 16 image-first named-person cards (11 wartime, 3 postwar/cultural, 2 context), plus the separate actual 1995 Мәншүк stamp. The old Әлия stamp remains in the asset inventory but is no longer her profile image. Every existing/new card was rendered and visually inspected at 360px; screenshots are in [review/topic8](review/topic8/README.md). No face is substituted for another person.

The registry retains exact original URLs, catalogue identities, captions/alt text, creators, record-specific rights, review dates and SHA-256. Original Commons files (including any crop already made by Commons) are bundled unchanged: no new face crop, colorization or retouch. Responsive object-fit:contain preserves whole stamps/coins; no hotlink dependency. `npm run check:media` verifies signatures and bytes. A temporary review-only download Action committed the reviewed originals, then was removed; it never deployed or accessed a backend.

## Visible quality/provenance limits

- Әлия: clear sniper photo, museum catalogue/newspaper provenance; Commons date range 1942–1944, no invented exact date. Мәншүк: clear identified 1937 portrait; explicitly not a wartime photo. Her 1995 stamp stays a separately captioned artefact.
- Бауыржан: identified Egemen archive crop, exact year unknown (before 1982). Талғат and Сағадат: clear 2009 veteran photographs; never captioned as wartime. Панфилов: identified 1941 photo, photographer Mikhail Kalashnikov/TASS and newspaper publication recorded.
- Хиуаз: visually clear identified portrait. Commons supplies PD-Kazakhstan/PD-Russia tags, but its immediate source is Fishki.net and author/original publication date are absent. Better archival provenance remains a gap, explicitly noted on the card; a search result is not substituted for that proof.
- Мәлік, Нұркен and Рақымжан: complete named official commemorative stamps, explicitly not portrait photographs. Better reusable standalone archive photographs remain desirable. A deleted Рақымжан photo with an undelete date of 2051 was rejected rather than copied.
- Төлеген: clear named museum portrait bust photographed by Nurken, CC BY-SA 4.0 on the Commons record. It is labelled a photograph of sculpture, not a photograph of the living person. The record does not identify the sculptor; fuller museum object/provenance confirmation remains desirable.
- Мұхтар, Қаныш and Абай: clear named stamp illustrations retained; not mislabeled as photographs or wartime participants.
- Бекмаханов: low-resolution 211×206 coin relief is correctly identified but the weakest phone likeness. A better reusable archive photo remains a gap. Filename/record date differ, so no issue year is invented in the caption; the National Bank catalogue was discoverable but its large PDF could not be fully fetched for independent page confirmation.

Candidate discovery used available web/image search, original Commons records, museum/state-catalogue and Egemen archival references. Google Images itself was inaccessible; no successful Google Images browse is claimed. Unlicensed museum/article illustrations and uncertain/deleted files were not used as replacement photographs.

## Historical maps, diagrams and source links

All 13 readings, 30 fact cards and 12 timeline cards have a relevant identified image or an original source-based educational diagram. Diagrams say they are learning diagrams, not archival documents; historical references remain directly beneath them. They repeat context visually, never add rubric facts. The [Natural Earth public-domain terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) were opened and checked. Atlas locations are approximate teaching positions with a text/button alternative; no period-specific border/frontline or troop-route claim.

The 18 text source URLs and all media record references were inspected using the available web tool. Primary Kazakhstan references support the term Ұлы Отан соғысы and current 9 May observance; no dead egov/Adilet homepage redirect was used in place of a cited document. Browser checks separately verify local image loading, alt text and source links in the rendered cards. They do not guarantee that a third-party URL will remain available. Full historian/rights provenance review is not replaced by HTTP availability.
