# Reviewed archive visuals — isolated draft

All seven originals were opened for identity and Commons reuse status on 2026-11-03, downloaded intact, inspected visually and bundled locally. `src/assets/archive/registry.json` records exact original URL, record, creator, caption, rights template, review date and SHA-256. `npm run check:media` verifies the bytes offline. Images use Vite asset imports, proportional `object-fit: contain` and no hotlink dependency. The earlier one-time review-only asset download workflow has been removed after recovery of both image commits.

PD-KZ-exempt is the Commons designation for Kazakhstan official symbols/signs including stamps and currency, excluded from copyright under the cited Kazakhstan law. US government Army work is recorded as public domain in the US. These are record-specific reuse grounds, not a claim that every old portrait is copyright-free. No archive photograph provenance is invented for a stamp or coin. The six commemorative portraits are explicitly captioned **«Еске алу жәдігері; соғыс кезіндегі фотосурет емес»**. Stalin's image is separately identified as an archival photo.

Every named human in the current reading collection has an image-first introduction card, then name, description, historical sources and image credit. All 13 substantive reading cards and 12 timeline cards also have a relevant artifact or original sourced diagram. Diagrams quote only the card's event context and retain its exact source references beneath the visual; they say **«Дерекке негізделген оқу сызбасы … Мұрағаттық құжат емес»**. Their author is Ел жады; no archival reuse permission is implied. Existing map outline: [Natural Earth public-domain terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/); coordinates are approximate educational positions, with no historical borders or troop routes asserted.

The requested Google Images discovery attempt was inaccessible in this tool environment. Identity, artifact inscriptions and rights were checked at the originals. Do not claim Google Images browsing succeeded.

The bank/facts are sourced learning material, not academically verified history.
